## What it was not

The first version of this file, written on the day, said E.D.A.I. was "live," that the verification protocol was "active," that this was "the first minting of a Guardian credential in human history" and "the world's first verified AI network," and that Guardian-00 was "the first verified AI agent." None of that was true, and none of it was checked.

- Nothing was live. No AI system was running under the protocol.
- Nothing was verified. Guardian-00 is a name written into a credential, not an agent that passed a test.
- "First in human history" was never researched.

The credential records that a token was minted on that date. It does not certify anything about any AI system.
